# F-001 · Word library & browse

**Business intent:** Learners in all nine IT roles (students, developers, testers, BA/PO/PM, PMO, IT HR, business development, sales, managers) get a searchable library of IT words at three levels, each with IPA, a definition, meanings in Vietnamese, Korean and French, a workplace example and audio. Partners such as BAPO Path can link straight to one role's words. · [intent](../intent/F-001.md) · [design](../design/F-001.md)

## Problem
IT vocabulary is spread across courses and glossaries, is rarely sorted by role or level, and seldom comes with pronunciation and a meaning in the learner's language, so learners cannot quickly find and practise the words their job needs.

## Users
- Guest learner
- Signed-in learner
- Content maintainer (edits data/ and runs the build)

## Done when
Done when every word in words.json has a term, part of speech, IPA, English definition, Vietnamese, Korean and French meanings, an example, a topic, a level from 1 to 3 and at least one known role; when a learner can find any word in Browse by typing it (or its meaning) in English, Vietnamese, Korean or French and narrow the list by level, role, topic and status; and when a ?role= link opens the site filtered to that role.

## In scope
- Word database built from data/*.txt by build.mjs, with definitions (data/definitions), French meanings (data/fr), IPA (CMU dictionary plus data/pronunciation-overrides.json), level overrides and role tagging (roles.mjs)
- Abbreviation cards: an abbreviation and its full form merged into one card (data/merge-abbreviations.txt)
- Role link (#<page>?role=<id>) and the focus bar with See the words and Show all roles
- Browse search across term, full form, English definition, Vietnamese, Korean and French, ignoring accents
- Browse filters: level, role, topic (business domains and IT topics), status (new, learning, known, saved)
- Select mode and bulk actions: mark as learnt, still learning, save, remove from saved, reset to new
- Listen buttons using the device's speech voices, with a slow button in the word dialog, accent and speed settings

## Out of scope
- Recorded human audio: all audio is the device's text-to-speech voice
- Word photos: cards show topic icons only (photos were removed on 2026-11-01)
- Bulk actions outside Browse (select mode closes when the learner leaves Browse)

## Risks
- Speech voices depend on the device; some devices have no voice for a language or accent
- Meanings and IPA come from data files and AI tools and may be wrong until a learner reports them
- The ?role= parameter only works inside the hash (#home?role=ba); a link like /?role=ba is only counted as a visit source and does not filter
- A guest who clicks Save or a mark button must sign in first, which may put off casual visitors

## Requirements
- **REQ-001** Word database with levels, roles, topics, IPA, meanings and examples — Done (Must)
- **REQ-002** Open the site for one role with a link — Done (Should)
- **REQ-003** Search and filter the word library — Done (Must)
- **REQ-004** Select many words and mark them at once — Done (Should)
- **REQ-005** Listen to words and examples — Done (Must)
