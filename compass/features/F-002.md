# F-002 · Languages & explanations

**Business intent:** Each learner chooses any of Vietnamese, Korean and French next to English and an interface language; meanings, example translations, plain explanations and stories then appear in those languages, with listen buttons for translated sentences. Non-technical roles see plain explanations on cards automatically. · [intent](../intent/F-002.md) · [design](../design/F-002.md)

## Problem
A single translation language does not fit a mixed audience, an English-only interface is a barrier for beginners, and IT definitions are hard to follow for HR, sales and managers.

## Users
- Guest learner
- Signed-in learner
- Non-technical learner (IT HR, business development, sales, managers)
- Admin (runs the AI tools that write translations, explanations and stories)

## Done when
Done when a learner can choose any mix of Vietnamese, Korean and French and see meanings (and, where the word has them, example translations, plain explanations and stories) in each chosen language; can switch the interface to one of their languages or to English plus their languages; and when HR, business development, sales and manager learners see plain explanations on word cards without changing a setting.

## In scope
- Meaning languages Vietnamese, Korean, French (any combination) chosen in My profile or the globe button; English always shown
- Interface language: English only, one chosen language only, or English plus my languages, from data/i18n.txt
- Listen buttons in each language's voice for example translations and story translations
- Plain-language explanations with an Automatic / Always show / Only in the word details setting
- Stories for complex words in the word dialog
- Preferences kept in the browser for guests and in the account for signed-in learners

## Out of scope
- Languages other than English, Vietnamese, Korean and French
- Listen buttons for meanings and for plain explanations
- Plain explanations, stories and example translations in the built-in words.json: they exist only after an admin runs the AI tools and they are read from Firestore

## Risks
- Translations, explanations and stories are AI-written and may be wrong until checked or reported
- If Firestore content has not been published or the AI tools have not run, no plain explanations, stories or example translations appear at all
- Devices without a Vietnamese, Korean or French voice read translations with the wrong voice
- Interface strings missing from data/i18n.txt stay in English
