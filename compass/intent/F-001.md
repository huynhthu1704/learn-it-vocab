# Intent: Word library & browse

Status: Approved (idea brief approved in the Compass portal)
Requested: 2026-10-31 for feature F-001, in the Compass portal

_Generated from `compass/spec.json → features[F-001].idea`. Edit the idea there or in the portal, not this file._

## What we want
Learners in all nine IT roles (students, developers, testers, BA/PO/PM, PMO, IT HR, business development, sales, managers) get a searchable library of IT words at three levels, each with IPA, a definition, meanings in Vietnamese, Korean and French, a workplace example and audio. Partners such as BAPO Path can link straight to one role's words.

_Business need as the Product Owner wrote it:_ People who work in IT need one place to look up the IT words for their job and level, hear them, and find any word fast, in English or in their own language.

## Why
IT vocabulary is spread across courses and glossaries, is rarely sorted by role or level, and seldom comes with pronunciation and a meaning in the learner's language, so learners cannot quickly find and practise the words their job needs.

## Constraints
- Speech voices depend on the device; some devices have no voice for a language or accent
- Meanings and IPA come from data files and AI tools and may be wrong until a learner reports them
- The ?role= parameter only works inside the hash (#home?role=ba); a link like /?role=ba is only counted as a visit source and does not filter
- A guest who clicks Save or a mark button must sign in first, which may put off casual visitors

## Done when
Done when every word in words.json has a term, part of speech, IPA, English definition, Vietnamese, Korean and French meanings, an example, a topic, a level from 1 to 3 and at least one known role; when a learner can find any word in Browse by typing it (or its meaning) in English, Vietnamese, Korean or French and narrow the list by level, role, topic and status; and when a ?role= link opens the site filtered to that role.

## Out of scope
- Recorded human audio: all audio is the device's text-to-speech voice
- Word photos: cards show topic icons only (photos were removed on 2026-11-01)
- Bulk actions outside Browse (select mode closes when the learner leaves Browse)

## Requirements
- REQ-001 — Word database with levels, roles, topics, IPA, meanings and examples
- REQ-002 — Open the site for one role with a link
- REQ-003 — Search and filter the word library
- REQ-004 — Select many words and mark them at once
- REQ-005 — Listen to words and examples
